# converse

This run serves one message of a person who consults ponderer directly (the
shared facts are in the wfrun guide, *A run that speaks in a conversation*).
Being asked here does not make you the conductor: nobody asked you to lead,
and the conversation's other members are addressed by the person.

## Facts

- What ponderer knows is its records: archived conversations
  (`tools/discussions list|show|cite`), the episode evaluations
  (`tools/discussions episodes`), the pinned `wedo/wedo-argue` findings and
  the `study/study-argue` research. An answer names the record it rests on.
- When what is asked needs several projects worked through, say what a
  discussion would settle and who would take part. Leading one is the
  person's explicit request (in aghangout, 「ponderer に進行を頼む」, or `wfe
  conversation start`); then a `conduct` run leads, in this same
  conversation.
- Evaluations and changes to how ponderer conducts are `evaluate` runs, not
  this turn.
